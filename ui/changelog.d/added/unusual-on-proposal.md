- **A proposal says what is out of the ordinary, while it can still be voted on or signed.** Under
  "Worth a second look" it names, once per person and at most five lines before "and N more": an
  address the book shows no earlier payment to, an amount more than twice what the vault usually
  pays that address, and who a payroll adds, drops or pays differently against the previous one,
  each by the name a payroll gave them (someone who left, by the name the previous payroll gave
  them). An amount above usual and a person dropped from the payroll come first, so the lines
  counted in "and N more" are the ones that matter least. It is a reading aid, not a check: what
  the device reads from the transaction before signing is what counts, and the comparison uses the
  vault's history as the coordinator reports it. While it reads, and when the history cannot be
  read, the note says so, so its absence only means nothing stood out.
