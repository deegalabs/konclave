- **The passphrase boxes of vault creation now have a visible name, and the rest of the screen reads
  better.** In the create dialog the two passphrase boxes were named only by a hint that vanished as soon
  as you typed; each now has a label that stays. The strength words (weak to strong) and bars were too
  faint to read on several backgrounds, the amber "fair" in particular; they now use the app's own text
  colours (the amber becomes a darker orange in the light theme). The edge of the create dialog's boxes is
  now visible. While you change your passphrase in Settings the three boxes lock and "Cancel" waits until
  the check ends, because the change goes through either way and used to report on a dialog you had
  closed. The warning that a backup came out incomplete now sits before the download and copy buttons.
  Not covered: other password boxes in the app (unlock, import) are still named by a hint only.
