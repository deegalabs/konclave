- **An invite link now always points to www.konclave.xyz, and the create and join screens show the
  address you are on.** Before, an invite made on any other address (a preview, or a lookalike copy
  of the app) pointed to that address, so the person who opened it landed on the copy and sealed
  their seat there. The create and join screens now say "You are on <address>. Konclave only lives
  at www.konclave.xyz", and on any other address (localhost aside) say so in words and in the
  warning colour. A new question in the FAQ explains how to tell the real app. The app itself still
  runs on any address, so nothing stops a copy from being served; this only stops Konclave from
  handing out links to one.
